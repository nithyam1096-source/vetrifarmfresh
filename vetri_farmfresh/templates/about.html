About Vetri Farm Fresh

Connecting farmers directly with consumers since 2024

Vetri Farm Fresh is an innovative agricultural e-commerce platform that bridges the gap between local farmers and consumers. We believe in supporting local agriculture, promoting sustainable farming practices, and providing fresh, healthy food to communities.

Our platform enables farmers to list their products directly, manage inventory, and reach customers without intermediaries. Customers get farm-fresh products at fair prices with convenient delivery options.
